About this plan

Draft plan: four days of Uyghur Kashgar, the desert at Dunhuang, a night with a friend in Lanzhou and the Tibetan monastery town of Xiahe. Then hitching south through Tibetan Sichuan to Songpan and Huanglong and the new railway down to Chengdu (and your birthday) with the Sanxingdui bronzes, Emei Shan and the Leshan Buddha, and Chongqing with the Wulong sinkholes, before the old caravan village of Shaxi and the trail high above Tiger Leaping Gorge. A hop into Vietnam resets the 30-day visa clock, then a week in the Yuanyang terraces and the Yao markets on the border. Night trains in hard sleeper do the long distances.
