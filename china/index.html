About this plan

Draft plan, slower version: the Pamirs, the desert at Dunhuang, Xi'an and Chengdu, then a long stay in the Dali countryside: your birthday in the quiet old town of Weishan, the Shaxi Friday market, the salt village of Nuodeng and the Erhai lake shore. A hop into Vietnam resets the 30-day visa clock, then a week in the Yuanyang terraces and the Yao markets on the border. Night trains in hard sleeper do the long distances. Prices in £, contacts for every bed.

Good to know7
  • iTrucks to the Kyrgyz post stop for hitchers, usually within 5–15 min and often free.
  • iAfter Kyrgyz exit the guards wave down a ride to the first Chinese checkpoint, then a shuttle (¥10–20 (£1–2)) takes you to the main hall. Immigration is done there now, not at Ulugqat as older guides say.
  • iHave ready: your hostel's Chinese name and address on screen, and the number plate of the vehicle you arrived in (it goes on the entry form).
  • !Full bag search: bags emptied, fruit and seeds taken, paper maps and notebooks looked through. Phones were checked for some people, not all.
  • !Taxis at the gate open at $100 or ¥600 (£67). People paid ¥100 (£11) to Ulugqat then a ¥40–50 (£5) minibus to Kashgar, or ¥200–350 (£22–39) in a shared van that leaves when full. In quiet season the van can wait hours, so expect an evening arrival.

  • !UK passports get 30 days visa-free, counted from the day after entry: in on Thu 5 Nov means out by Sat 5 Dec. The plan leaves Thu 3 Dec. If you only get in on Fri 6, the deadline moves to Sun 6 Dec and the plan still works.
