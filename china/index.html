Good to know

  • iSleep in Sary-Tash the night before. Trucks to the Kyrgyz post stop for hitchers, usually within 5–15 min and often free.
  • iAfter Kyrgyz exit the guards wave down a ride to the first Chinese checkpoint, then a shuttle (¥10–20 (£1–2)) takes you to the main hall. Immigration is done there now, not at Ulugqat as older guides say.
  • iHave ready: your hostel's Chinese name and address on screen, and the number plate of the vehicle you arrived in (it goes on the entry form).
  • !Full bag search: bags emptied, fruit and seeds taken, paper maps and notebooks looked through. Phones were checked for some people, not all.
  • !Taxis at the gate open at $100 or ¥600 (£67). People paid ¥100 (£11) to Ulugqat then a ¥40–50 (£5) minibus to Kashgar, or ¥200–350 (£22–39) in a shared van that leaves when full. Expect an evening arrival.
  • iBackup: a daily shared taxi goes straight from Osh to Kashgar for 7,500 som (£65), door to door (May 2026 report; organiser on Instagram as xieji86).
  • !UK passports get 30 days visa-free, counted from the day after entry. Miss Friday and the next chance is Monday.
