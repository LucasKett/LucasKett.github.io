    About this plan

    Draft plan: four days of Uyghur Kashgar, the desert at Dunhuang, the Tibetan monastery town of Xiahe and three nights with a friend in Lanzhou. Then Xi'an with Huashan (and your birthday), Chengdu, Emei Shan and the Leshan Buddha, and Chongqing, before the old caravan village of Shaxi and the salt village of Nuodeng. A hop into Vietnam resets the 30-day visa clock, then a week in the Yuanyang terraces and the Yao markets on the border. Night trains in hard sleeper do the long distances. Prices in £, contacts for every bed.
