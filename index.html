Irkeshtam pass

Fri 13 NovBorderKyrgyzstan → China

The pass into China, open on weekdays only. A long day: expect 8–15 hours from Sary-Tash to Kashgar.

Plan

  • Fri 13 NovKyrgyz exit stamp and a quick bag check, then about 2 km past the trucks to the Chinese gate. The shuttle is compulsory: ¥100 (£11) a seat, up to ¥400 (£45) if you're alone. Full bag and phone search at the first Chinese post, then 140 km to immigration at Ulugqat for your stamp. Shared taxi to Kashgar, ¥33–40 (£3.70–4.50).

Kashgar

From Fri 13 NovChinaChina

Old Silk Road oasis city. Your 30 days without a visa start here and run to about 12 Dec; each new entry gives a fresh 30, so a hop into Hong Kong from the south buys more time if you need it.

Plan

  • Fri 13 NovArrive in the evening.

  • LaterTashkurgan on the Karakoram Highway needs a free border permit from the Kashgar police border office: passport and one printed photo, usually same day. Shipton's Arch: £57–92 per car to split, plus £5 entry.
