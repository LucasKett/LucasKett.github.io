Tashkent

Fri 23 – Sun 25 Oct2 nightsUzbekistanDone

The capital. Most of the old city went in the 1966 earthquake, so forum posters give it a day or two. The Soviet-era metro stations are the sight.

Plan

  • Fri 23 OctOff the coach early. Chorsu Bazaar from 8:00 (tandoor bread bakers, dried fruit), the Kukeldash madrasa next door, then the Hazrati Imam (Khast Imam) complex before Friday prayers at midday. Plov at Kamolon Osh by the hostel. Evening: the new Centre for Contemporary Arts (free, register online first), sunset at Amir Timur Square and Hotel Uzbekistan, then Pakhtakor v Neftchi at 19:00, 1st v 2nd in the league.
  • Sat 24 OctMetro stations (Kosmonavtlar, Alisher Navoi), the Museum of Applied Arts (about £1.90), then Donat's free street-art walk at 15:00 or his Soviet Tashkent by Night walk at 19:30 (book on Guruwalk, tip-based).
  • Sun 25 OctBack to Olmazor by 10:00 for a shared taxi to Jizzakh and on to the Nuratau villages.

Tue 27 – Sat 31 Oct4 nightsUzbekistan

Irkeshtam pass

Wed 4 NovBorderKyrgyzstan → China

The pass into China, open on weekdays only. A long day: expect 8–15 hours from Sary-Tash to Kashgar.

Plan

  • Wed 4 NovKyrgyz exit stamp and a quick bag check, then about 2 km past the trucks to the Chinese gate. Ride with a truck or take the compulsory shuttle: ¥100 (£11) a seat, up to ¥400 (£45) if you're alone. Full bag and phone search at the first Chinese post, then 140 km to immigration at Ulugqat for your stamp. Shared van or minibus to Kashgar in the evening, ¥33–40 (£3.70–4.50).

Kashgar

From Wed 4 NovChinaChina

Old Silk Road oasis city. Your 30 days without a visa start here and run to about 3 Dec; each new entry gives a fresh 30, so a hop into Hong Kong from the south buys more time if you need it.

Plan

  • Wed 4 NovArrive in the evening.
  • Sun 8 NovThe Sunday livestock market.
  • LaterKarakul Lake and Tashkurgan on the Karakoram Highway. Since April 2026 the border-area permit is electronic and foreigners apply in person; reportedly 15–30 min at the Kashgar airport immigration desk. Foreigners can't sleep at the lake, so you sleep in Tashkurgan. From November there's one morning departure a day from the Tashkurgan County Liaison Office (16 Tiannan Road), and snow shuts the road some days.
